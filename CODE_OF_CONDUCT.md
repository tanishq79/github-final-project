Code of Conduct

Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who participates in this project.

Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Being respectful and considerate.
* Accepting constructive criticism.
* Collaborating openly and professionally.
* Respecting different opinions and experiences.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and abusive language.

Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related activities.

Enforcement

Inappropriate behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially to the extent possible.

Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take the following actions depending on the severity of a violation:

1. Warning the person responsible.
2. Temporarily restricting participation.
3. Removing access to the project for serious or repeated violations.

Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by community standards established by the Contributor Covenant.
